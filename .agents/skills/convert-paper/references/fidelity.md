# Fidelity rules

Preserve prose, reading order, equation tags, footnotes, captions, notes and appendix boundaries. Use rendered images for semantic equations; raw embedded text is a locator and can have damaged fonts. Check signs, scripts, fractions, summation/integration limits, and symbol identities.

For tables preserve row/column labels, panels, units, values, empty cells, standard errors, significance marks and notes. TSVs aid comparison but are not ground truth. Never infer an unreadable cell from surrounding results. Mark uncertainty explicitly and record the exact region.

Retain page markers. Do not paraphrase ordinary source prose, add referee opinions, invent an absent appendix, or treat plotted estimates as exact data. Corrections belong in reviewed copies, with source evidence and an audit record. Preserve deterministic outputs and original PDFs.
